- New AI 33: computer orks and space marines both changed. When behind on points they go for the victory points sooner and fight only where a victory point is at stake or at home; a lost power point is retaken first, and their own generators taken by the enemy are no longer knocked down. Tier 3 only when there is room and money for its squads, and every new tier brings at least one squad of that tier. Jump troops and teleports go only into a fight the army is already in, never under the enemy base. A hero under focused fire retreats at once instead of walking away - sometimes a bit early.
- Space marines: devastators and other heavy weapon teams are no longer moved every second, they set up and keep firing (sometimes they stay when the army leaves). Scouts get shotguns, the second scout squad sniper rifles; tactical marines keep away from melee; the techmarine takes his wargear early, uses his abilities and puts up his first turret sooner. Tier 3 is rarer now.
- Orks: a trukk carries slugga boyz to enemy shooters and to unguarded power points and back - it is often lost, and the orks lose more squads with it. Sometimes a painboy who heals. Our hero revives a fallen allied hero when it is safe. Two or more heavy weapon teams together are not attacked head-on while the orks are weaker; at tier 2 a deff dread goes in.
- Still to watch: stormboyz against heavy weapon teams were rarely seen on the bench, and "points first when behind" did not show there either. Tell us if the computer fights somewhere else while its points run out. Using cover before a fight is not done yet.
