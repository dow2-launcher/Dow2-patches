- New AI 30: the orks hold the map the way the old AI does. Fast squads run to the nearest points one after another, lost points are taken back quickly (a lost victory point on either side of the map), an enemy listening post on a point is knocked down before the capture, the army no longer stands on a point it has taken, and it lets a fight go when the enemy runs home. The second slugga and shoota squads get their first weapons earlier. The Repair Krew is bought only when the orks have a vehicle. On our test bench, in 36 games against the old AI of the other races the orks are now ahead in 23-24 (New AI 29: 8-9; the old AI playing orks: 22) and lose three times fewer squads than the old AI playing orks. Every other race plays as in New AI 23.1.
