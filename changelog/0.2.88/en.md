- New AI 29: the orks play for the map and the victory points. When behind on points they go for the points harder; one squad takes the far points while the army stands guard beyond the point being captured; they come back to defend their own points and retake lost power points. They field about as many squads as they have seen the enemy field. In team games allies no longer go for the same point. Squads at half strength go home to reinforce and come back; nobody chases a retreating enemy into its base. Burnas set fire to generators, listening posts and buildings with enemies inside; lootas keep their spot and use their camouflage; the warboss calls the boyz more often and uses 'Ard Boyz, Use Yer Choppas and More Dakka in fights; grenades are no longer thrown at our own squads, mines go in the enemy's way; gretchin with the Repair Krew repair vehicles anywhere on the map. Every other race plays as in New AI 23.1.
- New AI 29: a match where only the orks play the new AI now sends its replay to our site too (it did not since version 24).
